Experience & Technology

Technology serves the experience

The benefit comes first. The tool comes second.

Web 3D

A 3D viewer in the browser

GLB / glTF models for a fast look on phone and desktop, using model-viewer or a custom viewer.

Apple AR

AR Quick Look

A USDZ file places the object on iPhone and iPad, without a separate app.

Android AR

Scene Viewer / WebXR

Web AR on Android, with a graceful fallback when the device cannot run it.

Handoff
